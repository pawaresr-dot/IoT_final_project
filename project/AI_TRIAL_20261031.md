# Four-demonstration model and first autonomous trials

Merged 5,228 frames from four operator-confirmed successful human
demonstrations into 82 segments. Segment boundaries preserve camera gaps;
these do not represent 82 independent successes. Fresh ACT training completed
1,800 steps at `training_outputs/act_four_successes_20261031`.
Training-only 24-sample MAE: 2.443, 3.320, 2.525, 1.984, 0.145 degrees;
gripper 1.115 normalized units. Hold-position baseline is better on most
axes, so these errors do not establish useful autonomous performance.

The operator moved the elbow inward. Read-only telemetry confirmed elbow
2833, all torque off, no servo alarms. Restarted hold with measured goals.

First execution used repeated first-action replanning. It moved the body
but settled overhead without approaching or gripping the object. Interrupted
by operator tool Ctrl-C; the runner's finally block requested jaw_hold.
Corrected execution to use ACTPolicy.select_action and its action queue,
resetting the queue after camera gaps or jaw operations.

Second execution: `vision_state/ai_trial_20261031T185610`.
Completed 45 seconds with 400 logged predictions/commands. Body motion was
bounded to 12 ticks from latest measured positions per submitted target;
the controller retained its own step, lead, calibration and servo checks.
No jaw-close command occurred. Last 50 samples had only 0–3 ticks of motion
per body joint. Camera shows the keychain still on the table. Autonomous
pickup FAILED; the arm retained a monitored hold with no controller error.
The logs and periodic camera frames are retained. No success label was added.

Likely next investigation: excessive stationary portions and state-dominated
one-step predictions, plus object/start poses differing from the demonstrated
states. This is a hypothesis, not an isolated causal result. Train/evaluate
active motion segments and inspect future-action error before more rollouts.
